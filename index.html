I am a sound recording engineer, camera operator, and event coordinator currently based in Ottawa, ON. At a young age, I began playing the piano, and also eventually the organ. At school, I played the trumpet, trombone and euphonium. I recently graduated from McGill with a Master’s in Sound Recording. I am proud of my time management, clear communication, exceptional organizational skills, and an approachable, friendly personality while I work. During my free time, I enjoy playing card and board games, and cooking and eating food with family and friends.
